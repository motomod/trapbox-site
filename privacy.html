Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Trap Box ("the app", uk.co.motomod.trapbox) is a game. This page explains what data the app handles, who handles it and what choices you have.

No accounts

Trap Box has no account system. There's nothing to sign up for and nothing to sign in to. The developer does not collect your name, email address, or any other personal details through the app.

Your progress stays on your device

Level progress, star ratings, best scores, and your sound, vibration and theme settings are stored only on your device, using the device's local app storage. None of this saved progress is sent to the developer, to any server, or to any third party. Uninstalling the app removes it. The gameplay events described under Analytics below are separate.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Trap Box is free to play and shows ads between levels, and offers an optional ad you can watch to continue after a loss. Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show ads, Google's SDK may collect data such as your device's advertising identifier, your approximate location (worked out from your IP address), app interaction data (like which ads were shown), and diagnostic data, and may use this data for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. This collection happens on Google's side, not the developer's — the developer never sees or stores this data.

For details on what Google collects and how it's used, see Google's ad policy and how Google uses data from apps that use AdMob.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played, for example which levels are hardest and how often the optional ads are watched. It records these gameplay events: a level starting (and whether it's a retry), a level being cleared (with the stars earned and how much of the board you claimed), a level being lost (whether you ran out of lives or time, and how much you claimed), whether you used a continue on that attempt, and whether an optional "watch ad to continue" ad was watched to the end or skipped.

Firebase also collects some information automatically, including an app-instance identifier, your device model and operating system version, your approximate location (country or region) and usage data such as sessions and time in the app. This data isn't linked to your name or any account.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report so the problem can be fixed. A report includes technical details such as the stack trace, your device model and operating system version, the device's state at the time (for example free memory and orientation) and a Crashlytics installation identifier.

Remote settings (Firebase Remote Config)

The app uses Firebase Remote Config to fetch a few game settings, such as which ads are shown and how often the ad between levels appears, so they can be adjusted without an update. Fetching them uses a Firebase installation identifier.

Firebase services are provided by Google. See Privacy and Security in Firebase for how Google processes this data.

Consent (UK / EEA)

For users in the UK and the European Economic Area, Trap Box shows a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before requesting any ads, so you can choose whether to allow personalised ads. If you don't consent, or if consent can't be requested, the app either shows non-personalised ads or no ads at all.

You can change your choice at any time from the Privacy options button, shown in Settings and on the pause menu whenever it's available.

Who handles your data

The data described above is collected and processed by Google, as the provider of AdMob and Firebase. The developer can see the analytics and crash reports in the Firebase console, to improve the game, but never sees the data Google's ad SDK collects. The developer never receives your name or email address and does not sell any data.

Children

Trap Box does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It contains no chat, no user-generated content, and no way to enter personal information anywhere in the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be sent to info@moto-technologies.co.uk.
