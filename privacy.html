Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Trap Box ("the app", uk.co.motomod.trapbox) is a game. This page explains what happens to your data when you play it.

No accounts

Trap Box has no account system. There's nothing to sign up for and nothing to sign in to. The developer does not collect your name, email address, or any other personal details through the app.

Your progress stays on your device

Level progress, star ratings, best scores, and your sound/vibration settings are stored only on your device, using the device's local app storage. None of this is sent to the developer, to any server, or to any third party. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Advertising

Trap Box is free to play and shows ads between levels, and offers an optional ad you can watch to continue after a loss. Ads are served by Google AdMob. To show ads, Google's SDK may collect data such as your device's advertising identifier, app interaction data (like which ads were shown), and diagnostic data, and may use this data for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. This collection happens on Google's side, not the developer's — the developer never sees or stores this data.

For details on what Google collects and how it's used, see Google's ad policy and how Google uses data from apps that use AdMob.

Consent (UK / EEA)

For users in the UK and the European Economic Area, Trap Box shows a consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) before requesting any ads, so you can choose whether to allow personalised ads. If you don't consent, or if consent can't be requested, the app either shows non-personalised ads or no ads at all.

You can change your choice at any time from the Privacy options button, shown on the title screen and the pause menu whenever it's available.

No data sold

The developer does not sell any data. The developer does not collect any data of its own to sell in the first place.

Children

Trap Box does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It contains no chat, no user-generated content, and no way to enter personal information anywhere in the app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
