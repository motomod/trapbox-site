Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Shape Volley ("the app", uk.co.motomod.shapevolley) is a game. This page explains what data the app handles, who handles it and what choices you have.

No accounts

Shape Volley has no account system. There's nothing to sign up for and nothing to sign in to. The app never asks for your name, email address or any other personal details.

Your progress stays on your device

Your best level and your current checkpoint are stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. They aren't sent to the developer or to any server. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Shape Volley is free to play. All of its ads are optional rewarded ads: when a run ends, you can choose to watch one for an extra life or a checkpoint. Ads are served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising ID, your approximate location (worked out from your IP address), app interaction data (such as which ads were shown or watched) and diagnostic data. Google uses this for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention.

For details, see how Google uses information in advertising and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played, for example how far runs get and how often the optional ads are watched. It records these gameplay events: a run starting, every tenth level reached, a run ending (with the level, your best level and your checkpoint), whether an optional ad was watched to the end or skipped, and play resuming after an extra life or checkpoint.

Firebase also collects some information automatically, including an app-instance identifier, your device model and operating system version, your approximate location (country or region) and usage data such as sessions and time in the app. This data isn't linked to your name or any account.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report so the problem can be fixed. A report includes technical details such as the stack trace, your device model and operating system version, the device's state at the time (for example free memory and orientation) and a Crashlytics installation identifier.

Remote settings (Firebase Remote Config)

The app uses Firebase Remote Config to fetch a few game settings, such as when a checkpoint is offered and which optional ads are available, so they can be adjusted without an update. Fetching them uses a Firebase installation identifier.

Firebase services are provided by Google. See Privacy and Security in Firebase for how Google processes this data.

Consent (UK / EEA)

If you're in the UK, the European Economic Area or another region where consent is required, the app shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform when you first open it, so you can choose how your data is used for ads. If you don't consent, or consent can't be requested, the app shows non-personalised ads or none at all.

You can change your choice at any time: tap the gear on the aiming pad to open Settings, then tap Privacy options.

Who handles your data

The data described above is collected and processed by Google, as the provider of AdMob and Firebase. The developer can see the analytics and crash reports in the Firebase console, to improve the game. The developer does not sell any data.

Children

Shape Volley isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from them. It has no chat, no user-generated content and nowhere to enter personal information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy or the app can be sent to m@motomod.co.uk.
