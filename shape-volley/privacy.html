Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Shape Volley ("the app", uk.co.motomod.shapevolley) is a game. This page explains what data the app handles, who handles it and what choices you have.

No accounts of its own

Shape Volley has no account system of its own, and nothing to sign up for. The app never asks for your name, email address or any other personal details. The only sign-in is the optional Google Play Games sign-in for the leaderboard, described below.

Your progress stays on your device

Your best level and your current checkpoint are stored on your device, in the app's local storage. Uninstalling the app removes them. Your run in progress is never sent anywhere. The one exception is your best level: if you're signed in to Google Play Games, a copy goes to the Play Games leaderboard, as described below.

Sign-in and the leaderboard (Google Play Games)

Shape Volley has a leaderboard run by Google Play Games Services. Using it is optional. If you already use Play Games on your phone, Google may sign you in to the game automatically when it opens. Otherwise you can sign in by tapping your Best level. You can play the whole game without signing in.

When you're signed in, Google Play Games shares your Play Games profile with the game: your gamer name, your avatar and a player ID for this game. It never shares your email address. The app uses your profile only to put you on the leaderboard and doesn't store it.

While you're signed in, the app sends your best level to the leaderboard. It sends it when you sign in, each time your best passes another ten levels, and when a run ends. Tutorial runs are never sent. Google stores your highest score and shows it on the leaderboard next to your gamer name. Whether other players can see it depends on your Play Games profile visibility setting.

You can sign out of Play Games, or change your profile's visibility, in Google Play Games. Your Play Games profile settings also let you delete Shape Volley's Play Games data, including your leaderboard score, or your whole Play Games profile. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles Play Games data.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Shape Volley is free to play. All of its ads are optional rewarded ads: when a run ends, you can choose to watch one for an extra life or a checkpoint. Ads are served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising ID, your approximate location (worked out from your IP address), app interaction data (such as which ads were shown or watched) and diagnostic data. Google uses this for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention.

For details, see how Google uses information in advertising and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played, for example how far runs get and how often the optional ads are watched. It records these gameplay events: a run starting, every tenth level reached, a run ending (with the level, your best level and your checkpoint), whether an optional ad was watched to the end or skipped, play resuming after an extra life or checkpoint, a shot being undone, the tutorial starting, finishing or being skipped, and the leaderboard being opened (whether it was shown, sign-in was declined or it couldn't open). These events never include your Play Games profile.

Firebase also collects some information automatically, including an app-instance identifier, your device model and operating system version, your approximate location (country or region) and usage data such as sessions and time in the app. This data isn't linked to your name or any account.

Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

If the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report so the problem can be fixed. A report includes technical details such as the stack trace, your device model and operating system version, the device's state at the time (for example free memory and orientation) and a Crashlytics installation identifier.

Remote settings (Firebase Remote Config)

The app uses Firebase Remote Config to fetch a few game settings, such as when a checkpoint is offered and which optional ads are available, so they can be adjusted without an update. Fetching them uses a Firebase installation identifier.

Firebase services are provided by Google. See Privacy and Security in Firebase for how Google processes this data.

Consent (UK / EEA)

If you're in the UK, the European Economic Area or another region where consent is required, the app shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform when you first open it, so you can choose how your data is used for ads. If you don't consent, or consent can't be requested, the app shows non-personalised ads or none at all.

You can change your choice at any time: tap the gear on the aiming pad to open Settings, then tap Privacy options.

Who handles your data

The data described above is collected and processed by Google, as the provider of AdMob, Firebase and Google Play Games. The developer can see the analytics and crash reports in the Firebase console, to improve the game, and sees the leaderboard just as players do. The developer never receives your email address and does not sell any data.

Children

Shape Volley isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from them. It has no chat and nowhere to enter personal information. The only names it shows are the Play Games gamer names on the leaderboard, which Google manages.
