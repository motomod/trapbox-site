Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. Shape Volley has no accounts. Just open it and play.

Will I lose my progress if I reinstall?
Your best level and checkpoint are saved only on your device, so uninstalling the app, clearing its data or switching phones resets them. There's no cloud save.

What do the ads give me?
Ads are always your choice. When a run ends, you can watch one for an extra life, which clears the bottom rows and carries on from the next level. If you have a checkpoint, you can watch one to continue from your checkpoint. Late in a run without a checkpoint, you may be offered one at half your level. Closing an ad early means no reward.

How do I change my ad consent choice?
Tap the gear on the aiming pad to open Settings, then tap Privacy options. The button appears where consent applies, for example in the UK and EEA. See the Privacy Policy for details.
