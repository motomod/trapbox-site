About

Shape Volley is a one-touch arcade game for Android. Pull back on the aiming pad, or point straight at the field, and let go to fire a volley of balls. Every ball that hits a neon block knocks a point off it, and a block breaks when its number reaches zero.

Each level adds a new shape at the top and moves everything down a row, and you get one more ball every level. If a block reaches the floor, the run is over. Blocks get tougher the further you go, so it's a race to keep the board clear.

  • Pick-ups float on the field: double damage, split balls and extra floor bounces.
  • Checkpoints: clear the whole board and that level becomes your checkpoint, so a later run can carry on from there.
  • Speed up or end turn when a volley is taking its time.

There's no account to create. Your best level and checkpoint are saved only on your device. The game is free to play and supported by optional ads: you choose whether to watch one for an extra life or a checkpoint.
