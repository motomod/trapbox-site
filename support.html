Common questions

Do I need an account?
No. Trap Box has no accounts. Just open it and play.

Will I lose my progress if I reinstall?
Progress is saved only on your device, so uninstalling — or switching devices — starts you fresh. There's no cloud save in this version.

How do I stop seeing personalised ads?
Use the Privacy options button on the title screen or the pause menu (shown whenever it's available) to change your consent choice. See the Privacy Policy for details.
