About

Trap Box is a fast, one-touch puzzle game for Android. Drag anywhere on the board to draw a line from edge to edge — anything you cut off and don't catch a ball in is claimed. Claim enough of the shape before the clock runs out, or before you run out of lives, and you clear the level.

Over 100 hand-built levels run across five worlds, each adding a new twist: blocks that split the board, portals that teleport balls across it, splitters that turn one ball into two, and chasers that hunt your cutting line instead of bouncing at random.

There's no account to create and nothing to sign in to. Progress and settings are saved only on your device. The game is free to play and supported by ads between levels, with an optional ad you can watch to continue after a loss.
